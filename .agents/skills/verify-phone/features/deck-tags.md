# Deck tags

The owner's deck settings carry a **Tags** row that opens the deck's Tags page: each tag in use with its count, swiping open on **Rename** and **Archive** and with **Rename…**, **Merge into…** and **Archive** in its ⋯, then **Archived tags** with **Restore**. Every change stays in this deck.

## Reach

A deck you own, its settings, then **Tags**.

## Drive

[`deck-tags.yaml`](deck-tags.yaml), as `learner`, on Lezione 12, whose seed tags are verb, reflexive, noun and money: rename noun and take **Undo**; rename reflexive to verb, see the sheet say the two merge and its ✓ read **Merge**; take **Merge into…** on verb and choose noun; archive money and take **Undo**; archive it again and take **Restore** under **Archived tags**.

## End state

[`deck-tags.mjs`](deck-tags.mjs) reads the deck's tags back: noun on three cards, money on three, nothing archived.

## Gotchas

- A row reads **name, N cards**, and its ⋯ is **Options for name**.
- The ⋯ is iOS's own menu, so its rows are found by their labels once it has grown.

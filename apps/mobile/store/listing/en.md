### Description (4000)

```text
Lymi is a flashcard app for what you want to remember: concepts from a course, facts for an exam, vocabulary from a lesson, or useful details from your reading. Make cards from your own material and review them with spaced repetition.

Make cards from your own material
• Write a term or question and its answer. With your permission, AI can fill empty fields with definitions, examples and notes. Each field it writes is marked and stays yours to edit.
• Bring a photo of your notes, a PDF or pasted text. Smart add extracts terms, and you choose which become cards.
• Import decks from Anki, Mochi, Quizlet or Noji. Anki decks keep their review history.
• Share text from Safari or another app straight into a deck.

Review at your own pace
• Recall the answer, reveal it, then grade how well you remembered: Forgot, Hard, Good or Easy.
• FSRS schedules each card’s next review from your recall history.
• Choose a daily goal. The lantern and seven lights show the rhythm of your recent reviews.
• Review previously loaded cards offline. Your grades sync when you reconnect.

Keep the context
• Add examples, notes and pictures to make a card useful to you. Review from a term, a meaning or a picture.
• Organize your material into decks and sections.
• Learning a language? Japanese cards support furigana, Chinese cards support pinyin, and Russian and Ukrainian cards support stress marks.

Learn together
• Share a deck with a link. Everyone gets new cards as you add them and keeps their own review progress.
• Start with a ready-made deck from Explore.

Keep your cards with you
• The same cards and progress on your phone and at my.lymi.app on your computer.
• Export a deck or your library as an Anki package or a Lymi file.
• Set an optional daily reminder at a time you choose.
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show what is due.

Lymi is free to use and has no ads. The app is available in English, Ukrainian and Russian.
```

### Description (4000)

```text
Lymi is a flashcard app for the words and phrases you pick up in lessons, books, work and conversation. Adding a card takes seconds, and Lymi brings it back right before you'd forget it.

ADD CARDS WITHOUT THE CLERICAL WORK
• Type a word or a phrase. AI fills in the meaning, an example and notes, and marks each field it wrote, so you always know what came from your lesson and what didn't.
• Photograph your lesson's pages or pick a PDF. Smart add lists the terms worth keeping, and you choose which become cards.
• Share a word from Safari or any other app straight into a deck.
• Bring your decks from Anki, Mochi, Quizlet or Noji. Anki decks keep their review history.

REVIEW THAT FITS INTO AN EVENING
• Spaced repetition with FSRS, the modern scheduler Anki also offers.
• Think of the answer, turn the card over and say how well you knew it: Forgot, Hard, Good or Easy. There's nothing to type.
• Set a daily goal. The lantern's flame grows with each review and counts the days in a row you reached it. One short day a week rests the run instead of breaking it.
• Write a memory hook for a word that keeps slipping, or ask AI for a first draft.
• Review offline. Your grades sync when you're back online.

MADE FOR REAL LANGUAGES
• Any language you can type, in any script. Lymi reads most widely taught languages aloud.
• Japanese cards show furigana, Chinese cards show pinyin with tone marks, and Russian and Ukrainian cards mark the stress. Each fades as you come to know the word.
• Add a picture to a card and review from the picture.

OUTSIDE THE APP
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show what's due.
• One daily reminder, at the time you choose.

LEARN TOGETHER
• Share a deck with a link. Everyone who joins gets each new card as you add it and keeps their own schedule.
• Start with a ready-made deck from Explore.

YOURS TO KEEP
• The same cards and progress on your phone and at my.lymi.app on your computer.
• Export a deck or your whole library whenever you like, as an Anki package or a Lymi file.
• No ads.

Lymi is free.
```

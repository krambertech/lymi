---
title: Insights, and a fresh Today and Library
summary: Insights shows whether your cards are sticking, Today and Library are easier to read, and AI assistants can add cards for you.
---

Insights is a new screen that answers four questions: is it sticking, are you keeping it up, what do you know, and what’s coming. Each answer is one number with a plain sentence that explains it, and it already makes sense in your first week, before there’s much history.

## A calmer Today and Library

Today now asks one question: how many cards are due. The decks with due cards are named under it, and a week of lights shows how your last seven days went. When nothing is due, adding cards takes the lead.

Library shows each deck with its language, how its cards split between new, learning and known, and what’s due. Open a card to see everything about it, including its whole history.

## Your AI assistant can use Lymi

Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant that supports MCP, and it can turn your lesson notes into cards, find cards and tidy your decks. Grading stays yours: an assistant can never review for you.

### Fixes and improvements

- Settings replaces the You screen, and a menu behind your name holds Archived, the docs and sign out.
- Cards due at the same moment come up in a random order, so the order you added them in doesn’t become a hint.

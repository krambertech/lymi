---
title: Lymi in Ukrainian and Russian
summary: Use Lymi in English, Ukrainian or Russian, with meanings and reminders in the same language.
---

Lymi now speaks Ukrainian and Russian as well as English. The first time you sign in, it follows your browser’s language, and you can change it any time in Settings. Meanings and reminders follow the language you choose.

lymi.app has Ukrainian and Russian pages too, written for the people who read them rather than translated word for word.

### Fixes and improvements

- Lymi now says “cards” instead of “words”, since a card can hold a phrase or anything else you want to remember.
- A deck opens on what’s due, with its new, learning and known cards counted.
- Add your own photo in Settings, or keep the one from your Google account.
- The sidebar highlights only the deck you have open.

---
title: Share a deck with a link
summary: Turn on a deck’s join link, and anyone you send it to can study it with you, each on their own schedule.
figure: join-link
---

A deck can now be shared. Turn on its join link in deck settings, send it to your class or a friend, and they can join with one tap. Everyone studies the same cards, and each person keeps their own schedule and progress.

The join page shows who shared the deck, how many cards it holds and a few of its cards drifting past, so people know what they’re joining. You can turn the link off at any time, and the people who already joined keep the deck.

In Library, a deck someone shared with you shows their name under it.

### Fixes and improvements

- Your browser tab now names the screen you’re on, such as Today or the deck you have open.

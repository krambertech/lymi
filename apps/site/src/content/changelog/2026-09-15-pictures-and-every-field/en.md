---
title: Pictures on cards, and every field in the app
summary: Add a picture to any card and review from it, and edit every field of a card in one form.
---

A card can now have a picture. Add one when you make the card or later, and it shows with the answer in review. You can also review from the picture itself: Lymi shows it, and you recall the term or the meaning.

Each picture has a short description of what it shows. It’s what a screen reader reads, and what review shows if the picture can’t load.

## Every field in one form

Adding and editing a card now share one form with every field: meaning, example, pronunciation, notes, picture, language, source, tags and how the card is asked. On a computer the less common fields wait under More fields. On a phone, each one opens from a row of chips.

### Fixes and improvements

- Turn on Create more to add several cards in a row without closing the form.
- Close a form you filled in by mistake, and Undo brings it back.
- Notes can use bold, italics and lists.
- Menus in Ukrainian and Russian no longer cut off their labels.

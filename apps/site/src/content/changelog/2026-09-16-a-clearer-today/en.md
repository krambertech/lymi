Today now leads with what’s due and one Review button, with your streak right beside it. Below them are three short rounds for when you want to focus: the cards you forgot today, new cards, and the cards that keep slipping. If you study more than one deck, each has its own row with what’s due in it.

## Your daily goal keeps your streak

Choose how many reviews make a good day. Reach that number, or finish everything that’s due, and the day counts toward your streak. Every grade counts, Forgot included. The lantern’s flame grows with each review and is full once you reach your goal.

When you get there, the review stops on its own screen. Today’s count rolls in and your week fills in a day at a time. From there you can go over the cards you forgot, carry on, or call it a day.

## A second chance in the same review

A card you forget now comes back a few cards later in the same review, so you get another go while it’s fresh.

### Fixes and improvements

- A card’s schedule shows as a timeline, so you can see when it comes back and how each review went.
- Until your first review, Today shows a short getting started guide.

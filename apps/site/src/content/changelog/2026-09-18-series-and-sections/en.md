Two new ways to give your cards some order.

A **series** groups decks that belong together, like the levels of a course. Review the whole series at once, or one deck at a time. In Library, each series is a heading over its decks.

A **section** is one part of a deck, such as one lesson. Sections open one after another: once every card in the current section has come up and 80% are known, the next one opens. If the deck is yours, you can choose to start each section yourself, or open them all at once. Everyone who studies a shared deck sees the same sections.

Decks without a series or sections work exactly as before.

### Fixes and improvements

- A deck lists its cards like a glossary, with filters for state and sorting by when each card comes back.
- On a large screen, a card opens beside the list, so you keep your place.

---
title: Lymi is open to everyone
summary: Anyone can now create a Lymi account for free, with Google or an email address.
---

Until now, Lymi was invite-only. From today, anyone can create an account, for free, with no waiting list.

Sign up with Google, or with an email address and a password. If you forget your password, you can reset it by email.

### Fixes and improvements

- Send feedback from the menu behind your name: a bug, an idea or anything else. It goes straight to the people who make Lymi, and we answer by email.
- The privacy policy, terms and support page are written in plain language.

---
title: Ready-made decks in Explore
summary: Explore has ready-made decks you can add in one press, explained in your language.
---

Not everything has to start from your own notes. Explore is a collection of ready-made decks for languages and other subjects. Browse it at [lymi.app/explore](/explore), or inside the app under Insights. Adding a deck takes one press, and it lands in Library ready to review.

Each deck has its own page with its sections and every card, and a few cards you can try before you add it.

Some decks come in more than one language. When you add one, you choose which language its meanings are in, and it stays that way even if you change Lymi’s language later.

### Fixes and improvements

- Ready-made decks show who made them, in Explore and in Library.
- Every deck on a shelf is in view, with no sideways scrolling.

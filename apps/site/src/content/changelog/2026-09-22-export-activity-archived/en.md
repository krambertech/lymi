Three things that make Lymi easier to trust with years of learning.

## Export

Export one deck or your whole library from the deck menu or Settings. Choose an Anki package, which Anki and Mochi open with your schedules, history and pictures, or a Lymi file, which brings everything back into Lymi without losing anything. The download stays ready for a day. A single deck can also go to a spreadsheet.

## Activity

Activity lists everything that came into your decks from outside the app: cards an assistant added, what Lymi’s AI filled in, imports, the files you exported, and who joined or left your shared decks. Each row names the app behind it. Open one to look over the cards it wrote, then edit or archive any of them.

## Archived

Archived lists the decks and cards you’ve archived, and brings any of them back with one press.

---
title: Invite people to your deck
summary: Invite someone to a deck by email, see who has joined, and keep shared decks simple for everyone in them.
---

You can now invite someone to a deck by email. Lymi sends them a link that works for their address only, and if they don’t have an account yet, they make one as they join.

Deck settings has a People section with everyone who has joined, and everyone you’ve invited who hasn’t yet. You can remove someone or cancel an invitation from there.

If you joined someone else’s deck, it now works the way you’d expect. You study it, About this deck tells you what’s in it and who made it, and Leave deck takes it out of your Library. Only the person who made the deck changes its cards. Your own reviews stay yours either way.

### Fixes and improvements

- Text across the app is shorter and plainer, in all three languages.

Some days don’t go to plan. Now one short day a week won’t break your streak. If a day ends below your daily goal, it becomes a rest day, as long as you haven’t had another in the six days before it. A rest day keeps your streak going without adding to it.

There’s nothing to plan or switch on. The streak’s calendar marks a rest day with a dashed line, and Today mentions it the morning after.

### Fixes and improvements

- Lymi checks for a new version when you come back to the app, so an open tab doesn’t fall behind.
- The streak window has a tidier layout and a clearer title.

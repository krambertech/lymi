---
title: Reviews that run to the end
summary: A review keeps going until what you opened is done, pauses once at your daily goal, and your edits now wait for you when you’re offline.
---

A review now runs until everything you opened it for is done: your whole day, one deck, one section or a series. If you started from the Review button, it pauses once when you reach your daily goal, so you can stop there or carry on. Every ending shows the same screen with what you did today.

You can also review just one section of a deck.

## Your edits wait when you’re offline

Add, edit or archive cards with no connection, and Lymi keeps the changes on your device and sends them in order when you’re back online. Reviews already worked offline. Now the rest of your changes do too.

### Fixes and improvements

- On a phone, every screen has the same top bar and a clear way back.
- The review card shows the deck and section it came from.

The monthly bars in Insights are now a grid of days, with a column for each week and a row for each weekday, going back at least a year. Each day is shaded by how much of that day’s goal you reached, in the same three steps as the seven lights on Today. Point at a day, or tap it, to see how many reviews you did.

Your streak and Insights now read the same days, so they always agree. Reviews you brought in with an import show in the grid too, though they never count toward a goal.

### Fixes and improvements

- A card you keep forgetting no longer loops within one review. It comes back after longer gaps, and never straight after itself.
- After you act on something, focus stays where it was, so the keyboard and screen readers keep their place.
- Buttons are easier to hit on touch screens, and the phone’s status bar follows your theme.
- On an iPhone, form buttons no longer cover the field you’re typing in when the keyboard comes up.
- Changes made with the keyboard happen without animation, and Reduce Motion covers more of the app.
- Ready-made decks preview their cards the way you’ll be asked them, picture first where there is one.

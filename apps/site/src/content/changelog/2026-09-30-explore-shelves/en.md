---
title: Find decks by language and subject
summary: Explore puts decks on shelves by language and subject, with tags, a shelf bar and More like this.
---

Explore is easier to find your way around. Decks sit on a shelf for each language they teach, most decks first, then on shelves for subjects such as Science, Technology, Nature, Arts and Citizenship. A bar at the top stays with you as you scroll, so you can jump to any shelf. A long shelf shows one row, and See all opens its own page.

Decks also carry tags, such as Travel or HSK 1, and search finds them. At the end of every deck’s page, More like this shows related decks.

Each language’s Explore lists only the decks you can read in it: decks explained in that language, plus, on the Ukrainian and Russian pages, decks explained in English.

### Fixes and improvements

- Deck colours read clearly in dark mode.
- Line breaks in card text show on deck pages.

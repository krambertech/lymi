---
title: Links in your notes
summary: Card notes can now hold links, underlined text and crossed-out text.
---

Card notes now support links, underline and strikethrough, along with the bold, italics and lists they already had. Write `[text](https://…)` for a link, `++text++` to underline and `~~text~~` to cross something out. Links open in a new tab, so your review stays where it was.

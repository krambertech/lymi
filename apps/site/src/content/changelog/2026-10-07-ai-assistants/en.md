title: Lymi in more AI assistants
summary: Connect Lymi to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor or Grok, and turn your notes into cards without leaving the chat.
figure: assistant
---

Lymi now works with more AI assistants, and better with the ones it already knew. Send your assistant a photo of your notes, a transcript or a page from a textbook, and ask it to make cards. They land in Lymi ready to review, and Activity lists every one.

- **ChatGPT** has Lymi in its sidebar, with what’s due in each deck, and you can @-mention a deck or a card in a conversation.
- **Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini** show small views of what’s due, how a deck is doing, or the card you asked about.
- **Cursor and Grok** can connect too, and the Gemini app is named as itself when it does.
- **The Lymi plugin** gives assistants ready-made skills: turning a lesson into cards, tidying cards, practising, and a weekly check-in.

Each assistant has a setup guide at [lymi.app/docs/mcp](/docs/mcp).

You can now write a memory hook right under the card while you review, the moment you need one. Lymi offers a draft in grey: press Tab to keep it, or write your own. A draft you keep as it is carries the AI badge.

Once the answer shows, the card also has a small menu, so you can edit it, look at its history or archive it without leaving the review.

## Forgotten cards come back once

A card you mark Forgot now comes back once, about twenty cards later, instead of several times in a row. Forget it again and it waits until tomorrow. A longer gap is a better test of whether you remember it, and you no longer see the same few cards over and over.

### Fixes and improvements

- Easy on a card you’ve already seen today counts as Good, because you saw the answer a moment ago.
- Until you connect an AI assistant, the sidebar offers Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok, each with its setup guide.
- Assistant views keep to what’s due and one card at a time, rather than long lists.
- lymi.app no longer shows a cookie banner. Visits are counted without storing anything on your device.

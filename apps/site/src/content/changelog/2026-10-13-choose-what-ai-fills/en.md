title: Choose what AI fills
summary: Say what a deck is for, choose which fields AI fills, and give AI instructions.

---

When you make a deck, Lymi now asks what it’s for: a language, a subject or exam, or something else. For a language, AI fills the meaning and an example, plus notes or pronunciation where the language needs them. For a subject, AI writes a short explanation as the meaning.

In a deck’s settings, choose the fields under Fields AI can fill. You can also add instructions, such as “Medical French for nurses. Use the clinical meaning.” AI never changes what you wrote. It uses the cards you wrote as examples, so new cards match yours.

How cards are asked is now called Review mode. Each option has a short description.

### Fixes and improvements

- Search finds Japanese cards by their reading. Katakana, hiragana and half-width kana now match each other.
- Anki decks with furigana in the reading field now import as plain kana.
- Replaying a pronunciation starts right away.
- Explore shows how a deck’s sections open.
- Switching the period in Insights no longer breaks the chart.

---
title: Mark important cards
summary: Cards you mark as important come up about four times as often.
figure: important
---

Choose Mark important in a card’s menu, on the deck page or during a review. Lymi then shows that card about four times as often, both as a new card and in reviews. Its due date doesn’t change.

Only you see the mark. You can also mark cards in decks that are shared with you.

### Fixes and improvements

- With Reduce Motion on, the lantern, review and Welcome no longer animate.
- An open tab reloads when Lymi updates, instead of showing an error.
- Safari no longer reloads a page as you leave it.
- Welcome no longer asks its questions again right after you finish it.
- lymi.app has new pages for [exams](/exams), [medicine](/medicine) and [certifications](/certifications).
- lymi.app has new pages about turning [tutor lessons](/tutor-lessons) and [notes](/notes-to-flashcards) into cards.

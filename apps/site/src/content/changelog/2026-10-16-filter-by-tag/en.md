You can now use tags to find and group cards in a deck.

- **Filter by tag.** On a deck page, open Filter and choose a tag. Choose two tags to see cards with either one.
- **Tag many cards at once.** In Select cards, choose Tags… to add a tag to all chosen cards or remove it from all of them.
- **Manage tags.** A deck’s settings have a Tags page. Rename, merge, archive and restore tags there.

When you type in a card’s Tags field, Lymi suggests tags you already use and shows how many cards have each. Tags that differ only in capital letters, like “Verbs” and “verbs”, are now one tag.

### Fixes and improvements

- The end of a review now shows yesterday’s rest day, like Today does.

You can now import from Noji and Quizlet. Open Settings and choose either one under Import.

## Noji

In Noji, export a deck as an OFC file, then choose that file in Lymi. Each sub-deck becomes its own deck. Verbs inside Spanish becomes Spanish / Verbs.

Cards you already know keep their due dates from Noji. Each gap in a cloze card becomes its own card, and frozen cards are archived. Noji doesn’t export past reviews, so your Insights, daily goal and streak stay the same. The steps are in the [Noji guide](/docs/import-from-noji).

## Quizlet

In Quizlet, open your set, choose Export and copy the text. Paste it into Lymi. Then choose a new deck or one of your own decks for the cards.

Quizlet only exports terms and definitions. Pictures, audio and progress don’t come with them, so every card starts as new. One import takes up to 5,000 cards.

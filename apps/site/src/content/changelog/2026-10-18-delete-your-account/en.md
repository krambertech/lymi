---
title: Delete your account
summary: You can now delete your Lymi account from Settings.
figure: delete-account
---

Open Settings and choose Delete account at the end. The page lists what will be deleted and links to an export of your cards. Lymi emails you a code to confirm.

Decks that other people study stay for them. They show “Its owner left Lymi”, and no one can edit them.

### Fixes and improvements

- lymi.app/signup opens sign-up even if you’ve used Lymi before.
- The lymi.app top bar now has Sign in.
- While you sign up, the browser tab says Create account.
- Try a few cards on a deck page now jumps to the cards.

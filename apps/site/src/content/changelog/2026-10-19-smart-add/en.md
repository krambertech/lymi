---
title: Turn a lesson into cards
summary: Smart add reads photos, a PDF or the text of a lesson and lists the terms you can add as cards.
figure: smart-add
---

Choose Smart add in the add menu. Add photos of the lesson’s pages, a PDF, or paste the text, then choose Find terms.

Lymi lists the terms it found. Terms already in your decks are listed apart. Tick the ones you want, choose a deck and add them. Lymi then writes each card’s example and pronunciation.

Smart add is in beta, so it reads up to 5 lessons a day. A lesson it couldn’t read doesn’t count. The count starts again at midnight.

### Fixes and improvements

- Your menu now has What’s new, which opens this page.
- A section now opens once you know its two-way cards.
- In review, tapping the speaker plays the word instead of showing the answer.
- The hint under an unanswered card now shows only in your first days of review.

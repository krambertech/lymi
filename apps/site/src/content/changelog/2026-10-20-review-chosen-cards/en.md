title: Review chosen cards
summary: Choose cards on a deck page and review only those.
figure: review-chosen
---

On a deck page, open the deck’s menu and choose Select cards. Pick the cards, then open Actions to review the chosen cards that are due, or all of them.

Review all takes the chosen cards you’ve started and haven’t reviewed today. New cards stay in your daily review. You can review up to 200 chosen cards at a time.

To review one tag, filter the deck by it, then choose Select all. This works in decks shared with you too.

### Fixes and improvements

- Filters you set on one deck no longer carry over to the next deck you open.
- Every card on a deck page on lymi.app now has a play button.
- If AI stopped filling a card, Enrich works on it again after a day.

title: Furigana on Japanese cards
summary: Japanese cards show the reading over the kanji, and examples hide it over words you know.

---

Japanese cards now show the reading over the kanji as furigana. Lymi takes it from the card’s Reading field.

In review, the furigana shows while a card is New or Learning. Once the card is Known, it shows only with the answer.

Examples can have furigana too. Write the reading in brackets after the kanji, like 毎朝[まいあさ]. AI adds readings to the examples it writes. Over a word you know from any of your cards, an example’s furigana fades.

## Recordings from Anki

Importing from Anki now keeps each word’s own recording as the card’s audio. A pitch accent field becomes a line in the notes. A sentence translation gets its own line there too.

### Fixes and improvements

- A Japanese card’s audio follows its reading, so 上手 sounds as じょうず or うわて.
- Two Japanese cards with the same kanji but different readings are no longer duplicates.
- In review, the pronunciation now shows with the answer. Chinese and Japanese cards you’re still learning keep it on the front.

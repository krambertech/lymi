On the sign-in page, choose Continue with Apple. It works for a new account too.

## Search your Library

Library now shows decks as lists. Your own decks come first, then each series under its name. To search, choose the search icon beside Library, or use the field at the top on a phone. Search finds decks by name, language or series.

### Fixes and improvements

- A series’ menu now has New deck, which makes a deck in that series.
- Archived decks moved to Library’s menu.
- A new card’s form shows term, meaning and deck first. Add any other field from the row of buttons under them.

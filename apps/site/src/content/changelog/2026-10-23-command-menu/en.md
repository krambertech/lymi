Press ⌘K, or Ctrl K on Windows, to open the command menu. Type to search your cards, decks and commands, then press Enter to open one. On a deck, ⌘Enter starts its review.

## Add a card from any screen

Type a term of 3 letters or more, and the menu offers to add it. For Chinese and Japanese, one character is enough.

- Enter opens Add a card with the term filled in.
- ⌘Enter adds the card right away, and Lymi fills in the rest.

The card goes to the deck on screen, or else to the deck you last added to. If the term is already in that deck, Lymi opens that card instead.

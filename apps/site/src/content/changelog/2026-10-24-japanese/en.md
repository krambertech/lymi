Japanese has features in Lymi made just for it. In the welcome, choose A language, then Japanese. Lymi offers Read Japanese Kana first. Then come Japanese N5 Vocabulary Practice, Japanese N4 Vocabulary and Japanese Essentials.

## Japanese cards

- Cards show the reading over the kanji as furigana until you know the word.
- A native Japanese voice reads each card, following its reading.
- Kanji show in their Japanese forms, not the Chinese ones.
- Search finds a card by its reading, in hiragana or katakana.
- Two cards with the same kanji but different readings stay separate cards.
- Imports from Anki keep each word’s recording and its pitch accent.
- In the command menu, one character is enough to add a card.

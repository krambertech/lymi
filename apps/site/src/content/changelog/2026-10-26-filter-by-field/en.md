On a deck page, choose Filter. Choose State, Due, Section, Tags or Important, then choose a value. Each value shows how many cards it keeps.

Each filter you turn on shows as a chip. Choose the chip to change it, or ✕ to remove it.

## More actions in Select cards

Actions in Select cards now has Mark important. In your own decks, it also has Move to deck and Archive. Each one offers Undo.

### Fixes and improvements

- Actions in Select cards now work on more than 200 cards.
- Keys 1 to 4 now grade a card right after you click the speaker.

Open Settings and find Apps. Under Add an app, choose Readwise or Yomitan.

## Readwise

Paste your Readwise token and choose Connect. Lymi checks Readwise every hour. It works with highlights from Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books and Readwise Reader.

- A highlight of one word becomes a card.
- So does a passage with the word written in its note.

Choose a deck for each language, then turn on the books you want.

## Yomitan

Yomitan is a pop-up dictionary for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Choose Make a key for Yomitan, then paste Lymi’s address and the key into Yomitan’s Anki settings. To add a word, hold Shift over it on any page and press +.

### Fixes and improvements

- Imports now show progress for each picture and recording.
- To report a shared deck or card, choose Report deck or Report card in its menu.

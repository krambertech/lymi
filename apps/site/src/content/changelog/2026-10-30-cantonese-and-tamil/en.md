Cantonese and Tamil cards now show how to read each term, right on the card.

## Cantonese cards

- Cards show Jyutping over each character until you know the word.
- Examples show Jyutping too. It fades over a character you know with the same reading.
- A Known 銀行 fades 行 hong4, but not 行 hang4.
- 行 hong4 and 行 hang4 stay two separate cards.
- A native Cantonese voice reads every card.

## Tamil cards

- Cards show the reading over each word until you know it.
- Examples show it too. It fades over a word you know.
- You don’t need to type a reading. Lymi works it out from the spelling.
- AI ends the notes with the spoken form of a word.

## Adaptive pronunciation

Adaptive pronunciation is a new switch in a deck’s settings, for languages that show pronunciation on the term. It’s on by default. Turn it off to read the term and example without help. Pronunciation then shows on its own line with the answer.

### Fixes and improvements

- Decks in right-to-left languages, like Arabic and Hebrew, now read in their own direction.
- lymi.app has a new page for [learning Polish](/languages/polish).

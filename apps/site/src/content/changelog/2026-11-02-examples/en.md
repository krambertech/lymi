A card’s example now has its own translation. It shows under the example, in review and on the card page.

AI writes the translation when it fills a card. To add one to an older card, choose Enrich. To stop AI writing them, turn off Example translation in the AI part of a deck’s settings.

## Hear the example

Language cards now have a play button on the example. It shows after you reveal the answer, and on the card page. It plays only when you press it.

You can make 100 new example clips a day. Clips you’ve already played still play.

## Keep sentence audio when you import

A deck with sentence recordings, like Kaishi 1.5k, now keeps them when you import it. The play button uses the recording. Recordings don’t count toward the 100 clips.

### Fixes and improvements

- Memory hooks are now called hints.
- The hint field has a Draft button. AI writes a hint for you to edit.
- Grades from two tabs or devices no longer overwrite each other.

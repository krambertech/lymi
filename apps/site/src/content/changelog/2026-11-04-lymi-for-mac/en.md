Lymi now has a Mac app. [Download it](/download) for macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Sign in, and it has everything the web app has.

## Add a card from any app

Press ⌃⌥L in any app to open quick add. Type a term and press Enter. Lymi adds the card to the deck shown, and fills in the rest.

If you select text first, quick add starts with it. Without a connection, the card waits on your Mac until Lymi can be reached.

## Lymi in the menu bar

The lantern in the menu bar opens Lymi, quick add and review. To see how many cards are due, turn on Due count beside the lantern. To keep Lymi in the Dock instead, turn off Show in the menu bar.

These settings are under This Mac in Settings. You can change the quick add shortcut there too.

### Fixes and improvements

- Add a card now has a Hint button.
- You can now change your name in Settings, under Account.

title: Pitch accent on Japanese cards
summary: Japanese cards can show where a word’s voice rises and falls, as a small chart under the term.
figure: pitch-accent
---

Japanese cards can now show a word’s pitch accent as a small chart under the term. Each sound has a dot, high or low. A line after the last dot shows where a particle would go.

In review, the chart shows with the answer. It also shows when you open a card.

AI adds the pitch accent when it fills in a card’s Reading. You can add it yourself too. Type the number after the reading, like たべる [2]: that’s the sound the voice falls after. Use [0] for a word with no fall.

### Fixes and improvements

- Importing from Anki now keeps a pitch accent field in the card’s Reading, so it shows as a chart. A field Lymi can’t match to the reading stays in the notes.

Lymi is now on Google Play. Sign in with your Lymi account, and your decks and progress are there. Unlike the iPhone app, it’s available in EU countries too.

## Review on your phone

Open Today and choose Review. Recall the answer, reveal it, then grade how well you remembered: Forgot, Hard, Good or Easy.

Without a connection, you can still review the cards already on your phone. Your grades sync when you’re back online.

## Add cards

Type a term, and AI fills the empty fields with a meaning and an example. Each field AI writes is marked, and you can edit it.

Take a photo of your notes, or choose a PDF, and Smart add finds the terms in it. You can also import decks from Anki, Mochi, Quizlet or Noji.

Press and hold the app icon for Add a word, Review and Search.

## The same decks everywhere

Your cards and progress are the same on your phone, at my.lymi.app and in Lymi for Mac. At the end of a review, Lymi offers a daily reminder.

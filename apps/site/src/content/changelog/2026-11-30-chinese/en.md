Chinese has features in Lymi made just for it. In the welcome, choose A language, then Chinese. Lymi offers HSK 1 first, then HSK 2 and HSK 3.

## Chinese cards

- Cards show the pinyin over each character until you know the word.
- Examples show pinyin too. It fades over a character you know with the same reading.
- A Known 银行 fades 行 háng in 行长, but not 行 xíng in 不行.
- 行 háng and 行 xíng stay two separate cards. So do 东西 dōngxi and 东西 dōngxī.
- Pinyin typed with tone numbers, like ni3 hao3, is saved as nǐ hǎo. This works from any app.
- Lymi keeps the simplified or traditional characters you wrote, and never converts between them.
- Characters show in their Chinese forms, not the Japanese ones.
- A deck in traditional Chinese uses a Taiwan voice.

## Import from Pleco and Anki

You can now import your Pleco flashcards. Open Settings and choose Pleco under Import. Choose simplified or traditional for the cards. The other form goes in the notes. Each Pleco category becomes a tag.

Importing an HSK deck from Anki now keeps its pinyin as each card’s Reading. A traditional form goes in the notes. Example sentences keep their pinyin.

## Korean cards

AI now writes Korean pronunciation in Hangul, not in romanization. It writes it only where the sound differs from the spelling. For 학교, it writes 학꾜. Cards that already have a pronunciation keep it.

AI now fills the notes on Korean cards by default. They give each word’s Sino-Korean roots, like 學 학 in 학생 and 학년. A native Korean voice reads every card.

## Japanese readings from any app

You can add a Japanese card as 食[た]べる, from Lymi or from an AI assistant. Lymi saves 食べる with the Reading たべる. If you already have that card, Lymi finds it as a duplicate.

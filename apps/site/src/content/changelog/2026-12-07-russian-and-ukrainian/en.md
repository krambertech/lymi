Russian and Ukrainian cards now show where the stress falls. While a card is New or Learning, its word shows with a stress mark, like за́мок. Once the card is Known, it shows the plain word. The answer always shows the mark.

AI marks the stress when it fills a card, in the word and in the example. In an example you wrote, it adds the marks and changes nothing else. On a Ukrainian card, AI writes Ukrainian and notes when a word looks Russian but means something else.

- за́мок (castle) and замо́к (lock) stay two separate cards.
- A word you add with its stress mark keeps it.
- Search finds за́мок when you type замок.

## New voices

New native voices read Russian and Ukrainian cards. They stress the syllable the mark shows, so за́мок and замо́к sound different.

## Decks to start with

On Today, choose Russian or Ukrainian in Explore ready-made decks about. Lymi offers the alphabet deck first, then A1 words.

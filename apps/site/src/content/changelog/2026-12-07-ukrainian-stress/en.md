Ukrainian cards now show where the stress falls. While a card is New or Learning, its word shows with a stress mark, like за́мок. Once the card is Known, it shows the plain word. The answer always shows the mark.

- за́мок (castle) and замо́к (lock) stay two separate cards. So do ру́ки (hands) and руки́ (of the hand).
- A word you add with its stress mark keeps it.
- Search finds за́мок when you type замок.

AI marks the stress when it fills a card, in the word and in the example. In an example you wrote, it adds the marks and changes nothing else. It writes in Ukrainian and notes when a word looks Russian but means something else.

## A new voice

A new native voice reads Ukrainian cards. It stresses the syllable the mark shows, so за́мок and замо́к sound different.

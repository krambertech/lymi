Lymi, with a storm lantern

A hurricane lamp you carry with you. It's lit while you review, it flickers when a card lands, and it's the one drawing in the whole app that isn't a standard control. Everything else is plain, rounded, and quick. Light theme is the default; dark is a warm room, not a black one.

Mark

One drawing for the app icon and a simplified glyph for 16 px. The glass is tinted, the flame is the only pure accent, and the metal takes the text colour, so the mark recolours itself with the theme.

App icon

Three icon options for the home screen. The amber one is the loud choice; light and dark follow the phone.

Outfit 600, tracked to -0.045em at display size. The lantern sits on the baseline like a fifth letter. Lymi is cut from lyhty, the Finnish word for lantern.

Colour

Pure white ground with warm-tinted greys. Amber is the flame, the primary button, and the "due" count. Nothing else gets it. Flip the toggle at the top to see the dark room.

Where it's allowed to be playful

Five places, each tied to something you did. Everywhere else the app is quiet.

The flame flickers on Good

Grade a card Good or Easy and the small lantern in the corner flares for 300 ms. Again and Hard leave it alone.

Finishing lights the room

The last card fades and the lantern brightens with a soft halo. That's the reward. No confetti.

Unlit when nothing's due

The empty state is the same lantern, off. It tells you the state without a sentence of copy.

Days as little lights

Seven small lanterns for the last seven days, lit or not. A streak you can see without a number shouting at you.

Undo everywhere

Wrong grade, wrong archive, wrong AI suggestion accepted: a six-second Undo toast. Cheap mistakes are the real delight in a review app.

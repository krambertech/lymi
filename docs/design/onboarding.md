# Getting started

A new account starts on Today, not on a separate set of screens. Today carries a first-run guide until the first review, so the place a learner learns the product is the place they will use every day. Its job is to get them to a first review, not to teach the product or ask questions whose answers change nothing. The web drew Welcome's questions until October 2026; the phone still does (below).

## The guide

`FirstRunGuide` in `components/first-run.tsx` sits on the bare canvas, dashed like every empty state ([empty-states.md](system/empty-states.md)), and is a path, as on the phone: the lantern heads a line that runs down three numbered steps, lit amber as far as the step the learner is on. One column holds the lantern and the numbers and one the words, so the heading, the line under it and every step's title start at one edge; the line stops just short of the lantern's base and of each number. The heading greets the learner by first name and stays put, the line under it says what comes next, and the count ("1 of 3 done") sits in the corner on the heading's line.

1. **Create a deck**: done once the learner has a deck of their own, or any deck with cards. An empty deck someone shared does not count, because its member cannot add to it.
2. **Add cards**: done once a deck has cards. While current it holds **Add a card** and, where Smart add is allowed, **Turn your notes into cards**; the learner's own empty deck is named in its line.
3. **Finish your first review**: holds **Review**, which opens that deck's review. The guide leaves with the first accepted grade.

Every step has the same box, so the rhythm holds whatever is current; the current one is the one filled plate and holds its action. A done step is an amber check with its title struck through, the current step's number is dark, and a later step waits muted. There are no progress bars. While the guide shows, Today's own cards wait, because they have nothing to say yet.

## Under the guide

**Create your first deck** comes first, because most learners bring their own material. Three cards, each with its own button and an icon on it: **From your notes** (camera, file and paste marks; Smart add, which reads a photo, a PDF or pasted text), **Connect your AI assistant** (the Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok marks, each opening its setup guide; **Connect** opens them all) and **Import a deck** (Anki, Mochi, Noji and Quizlet by name; Settings at Import). Once an assistant is connected its card says so, **Claude is connected**, with what to ask it and **Connected apps** in place of Connect. **Or start an empty deck** opens New deck. Once the learner has a deck of their own the heading reads **More ways to add cards** and the empty-deck link goes; when their only deck with cards is one someone shared, it reads **Make your own deck**. The rail's assistant card stays hidden on Today while the guide shows, so the offer is made once.

The ready-made decks follow under a heading whose last words are the choice: **Explore ready-made decks about** **anything**, a language, or **tests**, underlined in amber and opening a short list the learner can search by the language's name, with each choice's deck count; **All decks** opens Explore. The list holds the catalogue's languages, most decks first, then **For a test**, with **Anything** for all of them. A language leads with the decks a beginner starts from, where `BEGINNER_DECKS` in `packages/client/src/welcome.ts` names them: Japanese offers Read Japanese Kana, JLPT N5, JLPT N4 and Japanese Essentials first, and one that is not published leaves its place to the shelf's next deck. It starts on the language the learner most likely came for: the deck they arrived with, else the language they gave Welcome, else everything. The decks sit in one row that scrolls sideways, up to twelve, faded at the end while more follow; a new choice slides the row in from its first deck. **Add** puts the deck in Library and opens its first review.

## Getting set up

Today's first visit before any review counts as getting set up: the browser saves `PUT /api/settings/onboarding` with no answers and `from: "today"`, which stamps `onboarded_at`, starts the goal at 25 unless one was chosen, and stops the phone from asking Welcome's questions. An account that has already reviewed is not set up from Today, so it keeps its goal and stays out of `kind: "today"`. `/welcome` redirects to Today. An account made from a published deck's add page, a join link or an invitation comes back from sign-in to Today (`?continue=1` on the door page), where that deck has ticked the first two steps and **Review** is the next press. A deck with no cards yet goes to its own page instead, which says it is waiting for cards, as does an account already set up.

## Motion

The first visit on a device plays once (`lymi-first-run-lit` in local storage). The lantern catches from its ember, its pool blooms from 120 ms and seven embers lift from 420 ms; the heading rises at 260 ms and the line at 360 ms, then the three steps 140 ms apart from 520 ms. Create your first deck arrives from 820 ms, its cards 80 ms apart, the assistant marks popping in on a spring, then the ready-made decks. Every later visit, and every visit under reduced motion, finds it lit and still.

## On the phone

The phone has not moved to Today yet; a linked phone issue follows this design. It asks Welcome's questions (what the learner is learning, which language, how much time a day, and how to start, whose ready-made decks follow `BEGINNER_DECKS` as on the web) and saves to the same `PUT /api/settings/onboarding`, so finishing or skipping on either app holds on both. The app's root decides it once per sign-in, on the rule the web's Today used before it carried the guide: sign-in stays on screen until the account is read, Welcome then fades in as the only signed-in screen, and Today first appears once finishing or Skip for now saves `onboarded_at`, then opens where Welcome sent the learner, such as the first review. How it works shows its three steps already lit, since lighting them one by one flashed on the phone, and the questions' titles are a step smaller than the web's. The primary is the `xl` button at the foot, Back and Skip for now are glass in the bar, and every answer is a whole plate with a tick. Bring your own leads with **Turn your notes into cards**, which makes the learner's first deck from the answers, named for the language or My notes, and opens Smart add on it. Import finishes Welcome and then opens Import over Today ([ADR 0036](../adr/0036-setup-runs-on-the-phone.md)); Claude or ChatGPT opens its setup guide over Today; Type my own cards opens New deck over Today. A join link or a published deck's link offers Join or Add to Library whether or not the learner is signed in; signed out, it signs up or in first, then joins behind a loading state rather than showing the deck again, and a learner who has not got set up or reviewed gets Welcome with that deck and the goal instead of the deck's page.

Today's getting started guide on the phone is a path: the lantern heads a line that runs down the three numbered steps, lit amber as far as the step the learner is on, which is the one plate and holds its action. Only the learner's own decks and cards finish a step; a learner whose only cards are in a deck someone shared sees that deck ready to review instead of the steps. A day confirmed as nothing due before a deck arrived still keeps the streak, but its line reads as reviews to today's goal once cards are due. Under it sit the Ready-made decks banner and **Bring what you have**: the offer to connect an AI assistant with the assistants' marks until one is connected, then Import decks as a row that opens the web. `apps/mobile/src/welcome` holds both.

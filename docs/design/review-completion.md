# Review completion

A review ends on a dedicated screen with the lantern, a heading describing the outcome, today’s review count, the seven-day display, the streak and the available actions. Closing review with X also opens it after at least one grade since the previous completion screen. [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-a-review-runs-to-its-end-and-stops-once-at-the-goal.md) defines the ending rules.

Use full-width actions in a stable order: Review forgotten, Continue, then Done. Done is always last and primary. Continue uses a secondary label for reliable context: “21 to goal”, “8 cards left” or “other decks”. Do not include a deck name, a count for rounds or an unknown remaining count. While the goal is open, show the main count as “4 of 25 reviews today”. Review forgotten appears only when cards are available and names its count in text, such as “Review 3 forgotten cards”, rather than a badge that could resemble a keyboard shortcut. With nothing due, place Add cards above Done.

Completion animates particles and background light when reviews were added; an ending with no new reviews uses half-strength background light. The sequence that first satisfies the day also highlights today’s indicator and increments the streak. The phone adds the corresponding haptics. [motion.md](system/motion.md#the-end-of-a-review) defines timing. Exhaustion cannot be confirmed offline or after a failed refresh; show the failed check and Try again instead.

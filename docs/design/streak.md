# The streak

The streak is how many days in a row met their daily goal. It is the length of a habit, not a score: it never grows faster for better answers and never nags to be kept. The one moment it is marked is the end of the review that turns the day, where it rolls up by one as the lantern's spark reaches it ([motion.md](system/motion.md#the-end-of-a-review)); nowhere else does it celebrate. Which days count is in [How a review works](../review.md).

On screen it is a plain number in tabular figures, secondary to the screen's content: never the largest number on the screen, a progress ring or a celebration. Its meaning never depends on colour or on a flame. Today is open until it ends, so an unfinished morning shows yesterday's streak rather than zero. A run of zero is never shown as a number on Today's card, in the streak modal or at the end of a review: they say **Start your streak**, or **Start a new streak** once there has been one, and a nothing-due day with no run says only "Nothing due today." The pill in the chrome keeps its 0, because it is a count in the bar rather than a sentence.

Today's streak card and review completion pair the number with the seven lights: the lights say which days, the number says how many. A day's light carries three steps of amber according to progress toward that day's goal, from the lantern's glass to its flame. Full means the goal was met or every available review was completed; the middle step begins at half the goal. The steps mix toward `--glass` rather than fading to transparency: a translucent amber lands on whatever is behind it, and in the dark room that is the same lightness as an unlit day, which would make "a little" and "nothing" one picture.

The run lives in the chrome as a pill with no plate of its own, the flame and the number on the bar: on the rail's first line on desktop, and on the phone at the end of the top bar on every tab, before capture. The flame, described in [Flame](system/brand.md#flame), says how far today has come; the number says whether the streak is alive. The pill opens the streak modal.

Today also has the streak card, a deliberate repeat because the bar stays the same on every tab: the flame and the run, one plain line on where today stands (how many reviews are left to today's goal, or that the goal is reached or nothing is due), and the seven lights, where today's light shows how far the day has come. The whole card is the button, and it opens the streak modal.

The streak modal is a place in ADR 0017's sense: over the page on a desktop, anchored near the top so switching views never moves its controls, where it ends in a close button, and a page over the whole screen on touch, where it arrives from the end edge and leaves by a back button that names the screen under it, or by a swipe toward that edge. Its open state is `?streak=true` on any screen, so Back closes it and a reload keeps it. The modal holds the run with one plain line about today, today's attempts against the goal with the longest streak and the days reviewed in one plate under it, and the month. In the month a day that counted is ringed in amber and a faint band joins the days that kept the run, so a run is a length you can see; a nothing-due day carries the band without a ring, a rest day carries it lighter between 2px dashed amber edges, and a day still to come wears a dashed hairline ring, because a day that has not happened is not a day that was missed.

A rest day stays visible but forgiven, the lantern turned low for a night. In the month its band has dashed edges, so rest never depends on colour, and an end with no kept day beside it, such as yesterday before an open today or a rest day on a Monday or a Sunday, rounds off in a dashed half-circle that cannot be mistaken for a future day's dashed ring. A month with a rest day ends with a key: "A rest day keeps the streak through one missed day a week." In the seven lights its glass has a dashed edge and still shows that day's progress step. The morning after, Today opens with a thin `plate-2` banner above the cards, **Yesterday was a rest day** and "Your {n}-day streak carries on.", which stays until dismissed or the day ends, and the streak modal's line says the same. Nothing counts rest days, counts down to the next one or warns about it, and the reminder does not change.

The daily goal is edited only in the streak modal, never on Today or in Settings; its choices are in [How a review works](../review.md).

Settings shows the review timezone as **Automatic** by default, naming the detected timezone, and takes an explicit timezone as an override. Choosing one makes the override clear, and returning to Automatic restores travel updates; the rules are in [How a review works](../review.md).

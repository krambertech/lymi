# Redesign the deck page around its words

**Status:** Accepted on 15 September 2026 and delivered in one pull request, in the order below. The deck page is for reading: an integration writes and tidies most cards, and a member of a shared deck cannot edit. Opening a card follows [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-interface-primitives-are-shadcn-components-on-base-ui.md), which this work amends for a side sheet.

## Done when

- The word list reads as a glossary at a 375 px phone and in the 880 px column, with long phrases, Cyrillic meanings and a missing meaning.
- The header is the deck name and two plates: today's due count with where today's goal stands and Review, and the deck's split between New, Learning and Known.
- Filters for state, due and lesson narrow the list, each active one shows as a removable chip, and four sorts set the headings. Counts in headings follow the filters.
- A card opens beside the list when both fit, as a side sheet over the page when they do not, and as a drawer on touch. `?card=` still opens it, Back still closes it, and J, K and Escape still walk and close it.
- `DESIGN.md` and ADR 0017 describe the new page. Ukrainian and Russian catalogs have no empty entries.
- `pnpm verify`, `pnpm deploy:check` and `pnpm test:e2e` pass, and the deck page is inspected at phone, laptop and wide desktop sizes in both themes.

## Work

### 1. Record the design

Rewrite the deck paragraphs under "Components" in `DESIGN.md`: the two plates, the glossary row, filters and sorts, and how a card opens. Add a side-sheet row for a place to ADR 0017's table. Complete when neither document describes the old plate, filter or panel.

### 2. Header and rows

Replace `DuePlate` in `views/deck-detail-view.tsx` with two plates. The today plate shows the due count, the line `Streak` already writes about today's goal, and Review; with nothing due it shows when the next card is back and Add card. The deck plate shows the split with state icons.

Replace the two-line rows with a glossary table. A row is the state icon, the term and the meaning; the date appears only under the When it's back sort. When a term holds other forms joined by " · ", the forms after the first are set lighter. Term and meaning wrap and are never truncated. Below 36 rem of list width, a container query drops the meaning under the term. The AI source mark stays in the open card. Give the state labels a plural form for a set of cards, so Ukrainian and Russian read "Нові" and "Новые" on the plate and in the filter.

Complete when the design system's deck screen and the local `learner` persona render correctly at phone and desktop widths.

### 3. Filters and sort

Add `DropdownMenuCheckboxItem`, `DropdownMenuRadioGroup` and `DropdownMenuRadioItem` to `components/ui/dropdown-menu.tsx` in both the anchored and drawer shapes, with browser tests for each.

Replace the state segmented control with a Filter menu and a Sort menu. Filters are state (New, Learning, Known), due (Today, This week) and lesson, taken from each card's `source`. Active filters show as chips that remove them, and Clear filters appears when more than one is active. Search stays beside them.

| Sort | Headings | Date in the row |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson (default) | Each lesson with its count, newest first, then No lesson | No |
| When it's back | Due now, This week, Later, Not started | Under This week and Later |
| Recently added | The day the cards were created | No |
| A–Z | None | No |

Filters and sort are local to the page and reset when the learner leaves it. Complete when an e2e spec filters and sorts a seeded deck and checks headings, counts and the empty state.

### 4. Opening a card

Measure the deck page's container. When it fits a 640 px list and the 400 px card side by side, draw the card beside the list as today. Otherwise open it through `Dialog` as a place: a new side-sheet shape on a desktop pointer, and the existing full-screen drawer on touch. A resize moves an open card between the two, except while a field is being edited or the Move sheet is open.

`WordView` keeps its content. Its header gains the close button in the sheet and drawer shapes, and the phone's full-page variant goes. Complete when `e2e/word-detail.spec.ts` passes at desktop and phone sizes and a new step covers the side sheet.

## Risks

- Below a 1280 px window the card is a sheet. The list keeps meanings beside words down to 640 px, so that is the narrowest it gets beside a card.
- Moving the phone from a page to a drawer stacks the Move sheet over a drawer. `e2e/sheets.spec.ts` and the WebKit run cover that stacking.

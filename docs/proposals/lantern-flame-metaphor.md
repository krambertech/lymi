# Lantern flame metaphor

This proposal captures the feeling and product meaning we want from the flame inside Lymi's existing lantern. It deliberately does not decide what the component should look like, how many formal states it has, how it is implemented, or the precise motion used to express it.

## The idea

The lantern carries a living fire. Remembering is not something a learner completes once; it stays alive through repetition. Returning to a word, including struggling with it, is how the learner keeps that fire going.

The flame should therefore feel continuous rather than binary. It can become quiet, gather strength, and settle again, but it should still feel like the same fire over time. Finishing the learner's intended work should not simply switch the flame on, because repetition was already sustaining it. Instead, completion should feel like the fire reaching a fuller, more confident presence.

## What we have aligned on

- The lantern itself remains Lymi's established symbol and should stay recognisably the same lantern.
- The flame represents continuity of remembering, not whether one answer was correct.
- Repetition feeds the flame. Forgot, Hard, Good, and Easy are all honest repetitions and should not create a hierarchy of visual reward.
- The flame should not go dark merely because no cards happen to be due or because a review session has ended.
- As the learner repeats during the day, the fire may gradually feel stronger.
- Reaching the learner's daily intention should feel like the fire becoming full and settled, not like a prize, explosion, or unrelated completion effect.
- The experience should remain warm, calm, and encouraging without turning the streak or daily goal into pressure.

## The feeling

The flame should feel alive rather than animated for animation's sake. Its movement should have the irregularity, softness, and continuity of a real flame while remaining quiet enough to live beside focused learning.

Changes should feel as though energy passes through one living thing. The fire may breathe, gather, or rise, but it should not behave like a progress bar, fill gauge, loading indicator, bouncing mascot, or game reward. The learner should experience a natural evolution rather than notice a sequence of interface effects.

The strongest moment should still belong to the same flame. It may have more presence after the daily intention is met, but it should not become a different illustration or alter the identity of the lantern.

## Motion principles

- Motion should be organic, restrained, and continuous.
- A repetition should gently feed the existing fire rather than trigger a correctness celebration.
- Several repetitions close together should flow naturally into one another instead of repeatedly restarting an obvious effect.
- The fuller completion moment should emerge from the flame's current condition rather than jump to a disconnected result.
- Ambient movement should never compete with reading or recall.
- A still or reduced-motion expression must preserve the same emotional meaning.

## What remains open

- How the existing flame drawing can express quietness, growth, and fullness without changing the lantern or logo.
- Which learner moments should be visually distinct and which should blend continuously.
- How streak continuity, a confirmed no-work day, active repetition, and completion relate without making the metaphor feel punitive.
- What combination of shape, light, rhythm, and motion feels most natural.
- How the experience should adapt across sizes, themes, static contexts, and reduced motion.
- How the eventual component should be reviewed, tested, and connected to product data.

These choices should be explored visually with the existing lantern as the fixed starting point. The proposal does not prescribe component properties, named states, numeric thresholds, animation timings, flame scales, or implementation technology.

## Guidance for the next exploration

Begin with the current lantern and logo as source material. Do not redraw, simplify, or reinterpret their silhouette as part of exploring the flame. The work is to discover how the fire within that lantern can carry this meaning.

Explore the experience as a sequence rather than a set of isolated icons: the fire at the beginning of a day, the subtle effect of repeated practice, the moment the daily intention is met, the quiet after completion, and a day when no review is needed. Judge each direction by whether those moments feel like one coherent living fire.

## Evidence that a direction is working

- The existing lantern remains immediately recognisable and unchanged in identity.
- Watching the experience makes repetition feel like tending the same fire over time.
- Difficult recall contributes just as honestly as easy recall.
- Completion feels fuller and satisfying without becoming celebratory, gamified, or abrupt.
- Having nothing to review does not look like failure or loss.
- The motion feels natural and quiet rather than mechanical, twitchy, or decorative.
- The meaning survives when motion is reduced or absent.

## Not part of this proposal

This proposal does not design or implement a component, change the logo or lantern artwork, choose an API, prescribe exact visual states, or integrate the metaphor into product screens. Those decisions belong to a separate visual exploration and implementation change.

Lymi Pre-Launch QA

Thirty-two findings from driving the running product through every persona, viewport and theme it ships with. Nothing crashed and no request failed — what follows is about numbers that disagree with each other, screens that never finish loading, and charts that a learner in their first week cannot read.

Personas fresh · learner · streak · backlog · long · polyglot, plus two accounts built from scratch through the interface
Viewports 320 · 390 · 768 · 1280 · 1920
Themes light · dark    Languages English · Ukrainian
Clean no page errors, no failed requests, no horizontal overflow, focus rings on every control

Fix before signups

Each of these is reachable on an ordinary path, and each ends with the learner stuck, misinformed, or unable to get something back.

LY-01 Before signups Archive

Archiving a deck is a one-way door: Archived is still “Coming soon”

Archive sits in the deck menu and runs immediately, with no confirmation. A toast offers Undo for a few seconds. Once it clears, /archived — the screen the learner menu links to, and the only place Restore is meant to live — shows an empty “Coming soon” state. The deck and every card in it are gone from the interface.

The API already has POST /api/decks/:id/restore and the same for cards, so the data is intact; there is simply no way in. The seeded learner account ships with one archived deck and one archived card that cannot be reached at all.

Steps
  1. Open any deck → ··· → Archive.
  2. Let the toast time out.
  3. Learner menu → Archived.
Contradicts
CONTEXT.md — “Archive: hiding a card or deck without destroying it. The only kind of removal the app has. Restore undoes it.”
LY-02 Before signups Today

“2 cards due” and “Nothing left today” sit side by side

Review in the morning, add cards from the evening lesson, and Today says both things at once: the due card counts the new cards and offers Review, while the streak card beside it still reports the day as finished. The same pair appears on the deck screen’s Today plate.

The streak card is reading the day’s stored outcome rather than what is actually reviewable now. This is the most common daily rhythm the app is designed for, so most learners will hit it.

Steps
  1. New account, one deck, three cards.
  2. Review all three to the end.
  3. Add two more cards. Reload Today.
Confirmed
Reproduced from a blank account built entirely through the interface; also visible on a freshly seeded learner.
LY-03 Before signups Review

The review meter measures the daily goal, so it is wrong in both directions

The bar at the top of a review counts toward the daily goal, not the cards in front of you (apps/web/src/client/views/review-view.tsx:88 — const size = round ? round.size : goal).

A learner with nine cards due opens the review at 0 of 50 and finishes at 9 of 50, with the bar a fifth full, on a day the app has actually counted as complete — the streak went up on that same screen. A learner returning to a 60-card backlog gets the opposite: the bar reaches 100% with ten cards still to grade.

Since a new account almost never holds 50 cards, nearly every first review ends on a bar that looks like failure. The rule that finishing every available card also satisfies the day is never stated anywhere in the interface.

Seen at
0 of 50 with 9 due (learner) · 0 of 50 with 60 due (backlog) · 9 of 50 on the completion screen of a brand-new account
LY-04 Before signups Library

A deck that no longer exists leaves the screen on skeletons forever

GET /api/decks/:id answers 404 {"error":"Deck not found"} correctly, but the deck screen keeps its loading placeholders indefinitely — no title, no error, no way back. Still grey after 15 seconds.

Reachable from a bookmarked deck archived on another device, a shared deck the learner was removed from, or any stale link. DESIGN.md already specifies the component for this: “A screen that failed to load uses ErrorState … the title says what failed, the line says the fix, and Try again retries.”

Steps
Signed in, open /library/doesnotexist.
LY-05 Before signups Library

Library shows skeletons forever when the network is down; other screens handle it

With every /api/* call failing, Insights and Activity both show “Couldn’t load … Check your connection and try again” with a working Try again. Today renders in full from cache, which is exactly right. Library shows three grey blocks and nothing else, and the rail’s deck list empties out at the same time.

On a phone with a weak signal this is the screen a learner is most likely to open, and it never resolves or explains itself.

Steps
Sign in, then block **/api/** and open /library.
Related
Same missing error path as LY-04 — both are the deck queries.
LY-06 Before signups Activity

Activity never shows what an assistant added, though the screen promises it

The route queries imports and exports only (apps/web/src/client/routes/activity.tsx — importsQuery, exportsQuery). Cards written by an MCP client, the API or the AI never appear. The seeded learner account has three cards an assistant added today — the fixture even comments “still unseen in Activity” — and the screen reads “Nothing has come in yet”.

The screen contradicts itself: its own subtitle says “Cards that came in from other apps, and files you took out,” while the empty state narrows to “Imports from Anki and Mochi appear here.” With integrations as a headline feature at launch, writes from outside the app land silently — which is the one thing this screen exists to prevent.

Contradicts
CONTEXT.md and AGENTS.md — “Every write records an actor through audit.ts — the Activity screen exists so nothing an integration does lands unseen.”

Insights, and what a first-week learner sees

The charts are honest about sample size in their captions — “nothing honest to report”, “one bad evening moves it” — but the marks themselves overstate, and two of the headline figures disagree with Today.

LY-07 Confusing Insights

Ahead counts review directions; Today counts cards. Both say “cards”.

On one freshly seeded learner: Today says 9 cards due today, Insights says 18 peak, Wed and “33 cards over the next seven days”. Exactly double for the decks asked both ways.

forecast() in apps/web/src/server/services/stats.ts:236 buckets rows from cardStates, which holds one row per card per direction, while the review queue returns one item per card per day. A card asked both ways is counted twice in the forecast and once in the queue.

Measured
GET /api/review/queue → total: 9
GET /api/stats/insights → forecast[0].count: 18
LY-08 Confusing Today

Today says “0 New cards — add some from your next lesson” while Insights says 3 new

The Today tile counts what is left in the new round after the day’s draw has already taken those cards; Insights counts cards in the New state. Both are labelled “new”, one click apart, and the tile actively tells the learner to add cards they already have.

Measured
GET /api/review/rounds → new: 0
GET /api/stats/insights → cards.new: 3
LY-09 Confusing Insights

Consistency reads “0 / 0 days” and draws nothing

Add cards but review none — the state every new learner passes through — and the plate reads 0 / 0 days over a large blank gap. RunStrip returns null when days.length === 0 (components/run-strip.tsx), so the strip disappears entirely.

It is a step backwards from the zero state before it: with no cards at all the ghost plate reads a sensible “0 / 30 days” and draws thirty empty blocks. Adding cards makes the card emptier and its denominator nonsensical.

LY-10 Confusing Insights

One day of history draws a full-width solid bar — it reads as 100%, not as one day

Consistency at “1 / 1 day” fills the whole plate with amber. Month by month at “1 of 1 day” does the same. Both are the same shape the app uses elsewhere for a progress meter, so a learner’s first visit to Insights looks like they have completed something.

The strip is scaled to the days that exist rather than to a fixed window, so the first day is indistinguishable from a perfect month. Drawing the run inside a constant frame — the thirty blocks the ghost state already shows — would fix Consistency, Month by month and LY-09 together.

LY-11 Confusing Insights

The Recall chart has no scale, and its line ends below the marker while the headline says 95%

Four things go wrong in one small drawing:

No axis
The series is four weekly buckets (2026-08-24 … 2026-09-14) drawn as a smooth continuous line. There are no dates and no y-axis, so it reads as a daily trend across thirty days.
Contradiction
The final point is 87.5%, drawn below the dashed 90% line, while the figure above it reads 95%. At a glance the chart says the learner has fallen under the threshold.
Hard edge
The area fill is a rectangle with a sharp bottom edge, not a fade, and its right edge stops short of the endpoint dot — clearest in the dark room.
Occlusion
The “90%” label carries an opaque chip that punches a hole through the dashed line and the fill behind it.
LY-12 Confusing Insights

The Cards bar becomes one solid block when every card is in the same state

A new learner’s bar is 100% grey (“8 new”); a week later it is 100% green (“20 known”). A single full-width block carries no composition — it reads as a loading bar or a meter at full. The chips underneath are doing all the work.

DESIGN.md notes this bar is “the one place the colour stands alone”, which makes the single-segment case worth designing deliberately rather than leaving as a fallthrough.

LY-13 Confusing Insights

Ahead: “peak” is jargon, no bar carries a value, and today is not marked

The headline is “14 peak, Wed” — a statistics word, and an unusual sentence shape in an app that otherwise writes in plain lines. The amber bar marks the busiest day, not today, so the day the learner actually cares about is the same grey as the rest. No bar shows its number, so only the peak is readable.

The Ukrainian catalogue carries the same jargon: “4 пік, ср”.

LY-14 Confusing Streak

The streak modal never shows the goal it is measuring against

The modal reads “Today ✓ 10 reviews” over a completely full amber bar. The goal is 50. The number 50 appears nowhere on the screen — it only surfaces after pressing the pencil, inside the goal picker.

docs/design/streak.md asks for “today’s attempts against the goal”. As drawn it is attempts alone, over a bar that is full for a different reason.

LY-15 Confusing Streak

Calendar days are announced as “goal reached” when the goal was not reached

Every lit day in the streak calendar carries an accessible label of the form September 1: goal reached, 8 reviews. The goal is 50. The day counted because every available review was done, which is a different fact and worth its own words — “all caught up”, say. As written, a screen-reader user is told the goal was met on days it was not, and the same conflation drives LY-03 and LY-14.

LY-16 Polish Insights

Month by month repeats Consistency’s headline and is a single bar for weeks

Consistency says “18 / 22 days”; Month by month, three plates lower, is headed “18 of 22 days” and holds two bars. Until an account is two months old the whole plate is one bar, and it says nothing Consistency has not already said. Worth folding in or holding back until there are a few months to compare.

LY-17 Polish Insights

The Recall ghost puts its “90%” label on the opposite side from the real chart

In the zero state the marker sits at the end of the dashed line; in the live chart it sits at the start. The ghost is meant to be the same component at zero, so the reference should not move when data arrives.

Numbers and words that mislead

LY-18 Confusing Navigation

The rail’s deck number means “due” or “total”, depending, with nothing to say which

views/shell.tsx:107 — {d.due > 0 ? <DueCount>{d.due}</DueCount> : d.total}. A deck with cards due shows the due count in an amber pill; a deck with none shows its total card count in plain grey. Both appear in the same rail at the same time.

A learner who saw “Lezione 12 · 3” in the morning sees “Lezione 12 · 21” after reviewing, in the same slot. In the Ukrainian account below, two of the three numbers are due counts and one is a card count.

LY-19 Confusing Today

“50 reviews to today’s goal” next to nine available cards

The line appears on the Today streak card and again on every deck’s Today plate, without reference to how many cards actually exist. A learner three weeks in with nine due reads that they are 50 away from a goal they can only get nine closer to. The rule that finishing everything also counts is never said.

This is LY-03 in copy form. Both would be answered by one sentence that names the smaller of the goal and what is actually reviewable.

LY-20 Confusing Onboarding

The default goal of 50 is out of reach for a learner’s first weeks

The picker offers Light 10, Steady 25, Keen 50, Intense 100, and every new account starts on Keen. A first deck is ten or twenty cards, so the goal is unreachable for weeks and drives the meter in LY-03, the line in LY-19, and the full-but-not-met bar in LY-14.

Worth considering Light as the default, or a goal that starts from the size of the library and grows. The picker itself is clear and well made — the issue is only which rung it starts on.

LY-21 Confusing Settings

Settings has no review timezone

The screen holds Account, Language, Theme, Daily reminder, Connected apps, API keys, Export and Import. There is no timezone control, and none exists anywhere in apps/web/src/client — the client only reports the device zone automatically.

The server route is built and documented (PUT /api/settings/timezone, “manual keeps the given zone until the learner goes back to automatic”), and both CONTEXT.md and docs/design/streak.md place the control in Settings. The day boundary decides streaks, so a learner travelling, or on a device with the wrong zone, has no way to correct it.

LY-22 Confusing Navigation

There is no search, and the rail’s search button is never rendered

views/shell.tsx defines an optional onSearch and renders a search icon button when it is supplied. Nothing in the client ever supplies it, so the button never appears. DESIGN.md describes the rail’s first line as “the app tile beside the wordmark, search and the capture button on that same line.”

There is a per-deck “Search this deck”, but no way to find a card without first remembering which deck it is in. Worth an explicit decision before signups: build it, or take the dead branch and the line in the design system out.

LY-23 Confusing Auth

The sign-in screen is still invitation-only

/login reads “Use the Google account that received your invitation” with “Need an invitation? Request an invitation” beneath it. If signups open without this screen changing, every arriving learner is told they need something they do not have. Flagging it as a launch checklist item rather than a defect.

LY-24 Confusing Settings

“Not available on this server yet” is server-speak in a learner’s settings

The Daily reminder toggle is disabled and explains itself with the word “server”, which is the deployment’s vocabulary rather than the learner’s. It appears whenever the VAPID keys are missing. Two things to settle before launch: confirm the production Worker has VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY, VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY and VAPID_SUBJECT, and rewrite the fallback line in the learner’s terms.

LY-25 Confusing Today

A learner returning to 60 due cards is told to add more

The backlog persona opens Today to sixty due cards, an unlit lantern and a broken streak — and an empty New cards tile reading “Add some from your next lesson”, with an Add cards action. The tile’s copy assumes an empty day. A backlog is the one moment to say something else, or nothing.

LY-26 Confusing Auth

An unrecognised app produces “Let An app use your Lymi?”

When the consent request carries no client, the fallback name “An app” is substituted into the heading, capital and all, and its first letter becomes the app tile’s initial: a plain A. The rest of the page handles the error well — the red line and the disabled Allow are exactly right — but the heading needs its own wording for the unknown case.

Polish and accessibility

LY-27 Polish Typography

Tabular figures pull deck names apart: “Add to Lezione 1 2”

components/button.tsx:20 puts tabular-nums on every button. Onest’s tabular 1 carries wide side bearings, so a deck name ending in a number gains a visible gap — 6.02px on “Lezione 12” at button size. Deck names like Lesson 15 or Unit 3 are the norm, so this shows up constantly. Scoping the numeric variant to the figures that need to line up would fix it.

LY-28 Polish Typography

Long compounds break mid-word with no hyphen, stranding single letters

Rechtsschutzversicherungsgesellschaften breaks as Rechtsschutzversich / erungsgesellschaften under review, and as …gesellschafte / n in the deck list, leaving one letter alone on a line. There is no hyphens: auto, and the card’s lang is not driving hyphenation. German, Finnish and Ukrainian are all in the seeded personas, so this is the everyday case rather than the edge.

LY-29 Polish Review

At 320px the review mode truncates to “Prod…” while the deck name keeps its room

The card’s top line is deck · mode · state. On a small phone the mode is the part that gets cut, so the learner loses the one word telling them whether they are being asked to recognise or to produce — while the deck name, which the review does not depend on, stays whole. Truncating the deck name first would keep the instruction.

LY-30 Polish Accessibility

Insights skips a heading level, and the review screen has no heading at all

Insights runs h1 → four h3s → one h2, so the four stat plates sit a level below a heading that comes after them. The review screen exposes no heading of any level, which leaves a screen-reader user without a landmark on the app’s central screen.

Everything else in the audit came back clean: every control has an accessible name, every image has alt text, focus rings are a consistent 2px on all seven screens, and no ids collide.

Insights
h1 Insights → h3 RECALL · h3 CONSISTENCY · h3 CARDS · h3 AHEAD → h2 MONTH BY MONTH
Review
(no headings)
LY-31 Polish Streak

Future days in the streak calendar look the same as missed days

In the month grid, 17–30 September are drawn in the same ink as 3 and 11, which were days the learner missed. Nothing distinguishes “not yet” from “not done”, so the end of the month reads as a run of failures.

LY-32 Polish Forms

Card fields have no length limit until submit

Term is capped at 500 characters and meaning at 2,000 in the Zod schema, but the inputs carry no maxlength and no counter. Someone pasting a long passage types freely and only learns on submit. The error itself is good — “Keep the term under 500 characters.” shown inline under the field — so this is only about saying it earlier.

What held up

Worth keeping in mind while fixing the above

  • Offline review is real. With every API call blocked, Today renders in full from cache — counts, streak, deck rows. That is the hardest part of the promise and it works.
  • The review screen itself. Reveal, grading, the queue, the keyboard path, long-text handling with the inner scroll and soft edges — all correct at 320px and at 1920px.
  • The completion screen. Lantern, count, lights, run, one button. Correct singular and plural, correct flame state for a broken streak.
  • Duplicates. Typing an existing term shows “sbrigare is already in Lezione 12” under the field before submit, exactly as CONTEXT.md describes.
  • Dark mode is a separate warm palette, not an inversion, and holds across every screen checked.
  • Ukrainian is fully translated with no truncation or overflow at any width.
  • Empty states — Library, deck, Insights, Connected apps, API keys — are the most finished part of the product.
  • Error copy where it exists is excellent: “Nothing has come back for a second look yet, so there is nothing honest to report.”

Two patterns behind most of the list

The goal and the day’s work are treated as one number. A day counts when the goal is met or everything available is done, but only the first has a number, so the second borrows it. That produces LY-03, LY-14, LY-15 and LY-19, and it is why a full bar can sit above “10 reviews” against a goal of 50.

Cards and review directions are counted interchangeably. A card asked both ways is one card to Today and two rows to Insights. That produces LY-07, and the same card-versus-state ambiguity sits under LY-08 and LY-18.
